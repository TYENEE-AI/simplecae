# Material and manufacturing inputs

Use the actual part/process material data, not the model's display color. Isotropic elasticity requires `youngs_modulus_mpa`, `poisson_ratio`, name and source. A known density enables fixed self-weight. `von_mises_yield` requires a justified ductile isotropic yield strength and a declared safety factor. Missing strength data permits elastic fields but suppresses strength/capacity and candidate ranking.

For directional materials in v2 or assemblies, use `elastic_model: "orthotropic"`, `engineering_constants` (`e1_mpa`, `e2_mpa`, `e3_mpa`, `nu12`, `nu13`, `nu23`, `g12_mpa`, `g13_mpa`, `g23_mpa`) and `orientation: {axis_1:[...],axis_2:[...]}`. Axes must be orthonormal; axis 3 is their cross product. The reciprocal compliance is checked for positive elastic energy. STEP solid axes are in its imported coordinate frame; assembly placement rotates material axes into assembly coordinates. Do not mix nominal isotropic E/yield values into this model.

The available directional strength model is `failure_criterion: "max_stress"` with positive `strengths: {tension_mpa:[X1t,X2t,X3t], compression_mpa:[X1c,X2c,X3c], shear_mpa:[S12,S13,S23]}` and `criteria.strength_safety_factor >= 1`. Each integration-point tensor is transformed to that body's material frame; signed normal and absolute shear stresses are compared against their own allowable values. This component-wise model excludes interaction/damage criteria such as Tsai-Wu and progressive delamination. Von Mises plots are descriptive for this material; the strength-utilization plot uses the actual criterion.

Capacity needs `calibration: {kind: "coupon_tests" | "literature" | "mathematical_reference", reference:"...", applicability:"..."}` plus honest physical conditions. Reference describes actual testing/data; applicability includes printing/process direction, temperature, duration and other relevant conditions. The shipped `mathematical_reference` records verify equations only. They cannot establish a printed part's physical strength. Infill microstructure, defects, creep, layer damage and manufacturing variability remain unassessed unless a separately validated effective model covers them; no universal PLA/PETG strength is inferred.

`validate --suite materials` solves seven three-mesh references: three principal directions, compression, 45-degree rotation and rotated isotropic equivalence, including full displacement/stress/strain comparison. See the CalculiX manual's ELASTIC engineering constants, ORIENTATION and GLOBAL field-output definitions at [the official distribution](https://www.dhondt.de/). The reference values and limitations are saved with each generated input.
