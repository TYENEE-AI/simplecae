# Explicit connected assemblies

Use `schema_version: "assembly-1.0"`, `bodies`, `connections`, `supports`, `loads`, `criteria`, `mesh` and `conditions`. Runnable cases are in `examples/assemblies`; [case.schema.json](../assets/schemas/case.schema.json) contains syntax. A CAD assembly or overlapping visual shapes do not imply mechanical bonding. Each body has a unique name, model and material; optional `placement: {translation_mm,rpy_rad}` and positive `mesh_size_factor`. Each body gets an independent C3D10 mesh. No fusion or collision-shape substitution occurs.

Inspect a compound STEP with `inspect --all-bodies` and select each imported occurrence by `model.solid_id: {id,geometry_sha256}`. Select GLB occurrences by `scene_node`. Both require actual material assignment. The saved SimpleCADAPI reference uses the same part definition twice and verifies two distinct placed STEP solids. CAD tags/assembly constraints help identify geometry; mechanical connections are specified separately.

Support/load items include `body`. Selectors are in that body's imported local coordinates; total forces, couples and application points are in the final assembly world frame (N, N mm, mm). Each connection has `name`, `type`, and `master`/`slave` objects `{body,selector}`. Bonded and bilateral compliant interfaces require coincident planar patches with straight-sided quadratic triangles and identical footprints. Explicit `frictionless_contact` or `frictional_contact` use opposing planar patches with a declared geometric gap and no automatic capacity extrapolation. Equivalent axial `bolts` support preload locking; read [contact and section transfer](contact-and-transfer.md). Supported opt-in [contained planar CAD contact and curved planar bolt ports](contained-planar-contact.md); curved nonplanar contact remains unsupported. Source bodies must not have positive volume penetration; STEP uses an isolated OCC intersection check, mixed meshes use Manifold on their analysis boundary. This check never replaces source geometry.

- `bonded`: exact ideal no-slip/no-separation kinematics, using independently implemented segmented dual P2 transfer. Tests preserve rigid motions and constant traction on nonmatching grids. Bond strength, adhesive/weld failure and separation remain unassessed.
- `linear_interface`: positive `normal_stiffness_n_mm3`, `tangential_stiffness_n_mm3`, world `normal` unit vector and `calibration: {reference,applicability}`. These are distributed stiffnesses (traction/displacement), not total joint stiffness. Positive quadrature springs represent finite normal/tangential compliance. They remain active in tension and compression; this is not unilateral contact, friction, bolt preload, damage or adhesive strength.

`conditions.connection_parameters_verified` must be honestly declared. Missing compliance calibration suppresses capacity even when fields solve. Disconnected load paths, conflicting dependent constraints, patch/support conflicts, incomplete overlaps and excessive quadrature are rejected. A spanning load path does not by itself prove a physically correct fixture; equilibrium, source checks, full-field coverage and mesh convergence still apply.

Gravity is fixed on every density-defined body. Strength uses each body's own material/criterion, including directional axes; the weakest body's actual utilization governs. Elastic buckling currently qualifies bonded planar assemblies and single bodies. Compliant-interface buckling is explicitly rejected pending an independent qualification reference. Nonlinear collapse, imperfection sensitivity and changing contact are not predicted.

`validate --suite assemblies` covers uniform traction, interface axial jump, compliant bending, different materials, rotations, genuine SimpleCADAPI compound selection and bonded Euler buckling. The [dual-space mortar paper](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/S0036142999350929) informs the formulation; passing the shipped patch references does not establish all theoretical convergence results for arbitrary meshes.
